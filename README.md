# Expense Sharing App

## About

The Expense Sharing App is designed to simplify the management of shared expenses among friends and roommates.

## Problem Statement

Managing shared expenses manually can lead to confusion about who paid, who owes money, and how much each person needs to pay.

## Objectives

- Simplify shared expense management
- Automatically calculate individual shares
- Track who owes whom
- Maintain expense history
- Provide payment reminders

## Main Features

- Group creation
- Member management
- Expense management
- Automatic expense splitting
- Balance tracking
- Payment reminders
- Expense history
